Air-Fryer Baked Potato

Crisp-skinned, fluffy-centered potato with almost no effort. A blank canvas for butter, stew, beans, fish, cheese, or whatever the night demands.

Category: Sides
Yield: 1 potato
Prep: 3 minutes
Cook: 45-65 minutes
Equipment: Ninja Foodi Grill or air fryer; fork; tongs
Tags: working draft

Ingredients

  • 1 medium to large russet potato
  • Oil, optional
  • Salt, optional

Instructions

  1. Scrub the potato and pierce it several times with a fork.
  2. Oil and salt the skin if desired.
  3. Air fry at 400°F for 45-65 minutes depending on size.
  4. Turn once if convenient.
  5. The potato is done when a knife slides through the center easily.
  6. Rest a few minutes before splitting open.

Notes

For a giant potato, expect the long end. For a smaller potato, start checking around 40-45 minutes.
