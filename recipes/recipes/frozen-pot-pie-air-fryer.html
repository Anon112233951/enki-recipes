Frozen Pot Pie or Frozen Meal in an Air Fryer

A patience-first frozen-meal method for when the goal is hot, safe, and not scorched. The flavor stays cozy and familiar: flaky edges, creamy center, and lava-level filling if you do not let it rest.

Category: Main Dishes
Yield: 1 frozen meal
Prep: 2 minutes
Cook: 50-75 minutes
Equipment: Ninja Foodi Grill or air fryer; oven-safe dish if needed; foil; thermometer if available
Tags: working draft

Ingredients

  • 1 frozen pot pie or oven-style frozen meal
  • Foil, optional, for shielding edges or loosely covering the top

Instructions

  1. Remove any plastic film unless the package specifically says it is oven-safe and you are confident your cooker allows it.
  2. Use the original tray only if it is oven-safe. If unsure, transfer the food to an oven-safe dish.
  3. Cook at 325°F to 350°F until the center is bubbling hot.
  4. For a meal frozen hard in a small hotel freezer, expect 50-75 minutes instead of trusting the shortest oven time.
  5. If the top browns too fast, shield loosely with foil.
  6. Rest 5-10 minutes before eating.

Notes

Plastic film is usually a bad idea in an air fryer. When in doubt, remove it and cover loosely with foil instead. First bites can absolutely incinerate you.
