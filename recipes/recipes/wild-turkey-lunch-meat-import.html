Clean Wild Turkey Breast Lunch Meat

Clean homemade wild turkey breast lunch meat made from wild turkey breast, dry brined, gently cooked in a Ninja Foodi Grill, chilled, and sliced for sandwiches.

Status: Working Draft

Yield: About 1 to 1 1/4 lb sliced turkey

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Inactive Time: 8 to 24 hours dry brine, plus 4 hours minimum chill time

Cook Time: 35 to 70 minutes

Total Time: Best quality requires overnight dry brine plus overnight chill

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb wild turkey breast, trimmed
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp paprika, optional
  • 1/4 tsp dried thyme or poultry seasoning, optional
  • 1 tsp olive oil or melted butter, optional
  • 1/2 tsp Dijon mustard, optional
  • A few drops Worcestershire sauce, optional

Directions

  1. Inspect the wild turkey breast carefully. Remove shot pellets, feather bits, bruised or bloodshot meat, silver skin, hard tendons, ragged wound-channel meat, and any jelly-like or metallic-smelling damaged tissue. Pat dry with paper towels.
  2. Mix the kosher salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and optional paprika or thyme. Coat the turkey evenly on all sides. Place on a plate or tray, cover, and refrigerate for 8 to 24 hours.
  3. Shape the turkey for slicing. If the breast is uneven, fold the thinner end under the thicker section to create a more even roast shape. Tie lightly with butcher twine if available. Without twine, place the turkey on parchment, roll or fold it into a compact shape, wrap in parchment, then wrap in foil and crimp the ends.
  4. Set the Ninja Foodi Grill to Bake or Roast at 250 degrees F. Place the wrapped turkey in the grill. Cook until the thickest center reaches 160 to 165 degrees F internal temperature. For maximum safety, use 165 degrees F as the final target.
  5. Remove the turkey from the Ninja Foodi Grill. Leave it wrapped and rest at room temperature for 15 to 20 minutes so the juices settle and the center evens out.
  6. Place the wrapped turkey in the refrigerator. Chill at least 4 hours, preferably overnight. Do not slice hot if the goal is lunch meat.
  7. Unwrap the chilled turkey and slice thinly across the grain. Use for sandwiches, wraps, salads, crackers, or protein plates.

Cookware

  • Ninja Foodi Grill with Bake or Roast setting
  • Digital instant-read thermometer
  • Cutting board
  • Sharp knife
  • Measuring spoons
  • Parchment paper
  • Aluminum foil
  • Small tray or plate
  • Optional butcher twine
  • Optional deli slicer or very sharp slicing knife

Notes

  • Wild turkey breast is lean and can dry out quickly. Gentle heat and controlled internal temperature matter more than the clock.
  • This recipe starts with 1/2 tsp kosher salt per pound. For 1 1/2 lb turkey, that equals 3/4 tsp kosher salt total.
  • For a stronger deli-meat flavor next time, increase to 3/4 tsp kosher salt per pound. For 1 1/2 lb turkey, that equals about 1 1/8 tsp kosher salt total.
  • The Ninja Foodi Grill is preferred over pressure cooking because it gives a slower, more controlled heat rise and a firmer, more sliceable lunch-meat texture.
  • Pressure cooking is better for shredded turkey, soup meat, gravy meat, turkey salad, or pulled-style turkey.
  • Store refrigerated in an airtight container. Best quality is 3 to 4 days. Freeze small portions for longer storage.
  • Food safety target is based on USDA poultry guidance: 165 degrees F internal temperature.
