Pork Chops with Carrots and Celery

A plainspoken pork-chop supper with sweet carrots, earthy celery, and enough browned butter richness to make a low-energy meal feel intentional. Mild, savory, and flexible enough to season after cooking.

Category: Main Dishes
Yield: 1-2 servings
Prep: 5 minutes
Cook: 22-30 minutes
Equipment: Ninja Foodi Grill or air fryer; air-fryer-safe parchment if used; tongs; instant-read thermometer
Tags: working draft

Ingredients

  • 2 thick-cut pork chops, about 1 inch thick
  • 2-3 carrots, rough chopped
  • 2-3 celery ribs, rough chopped
  • 1/2-1 tablespoon butter per pork chop, optional
  • Salt, pepper, Worcestershire, soy sauce, or preferred seasoning after cooking

Instructions

  1. Place air-fryer-safe parchment in the basket or pan if your unit allows it safely.
  2. Lay pork chops down first. Scatter carrots and celery around them.
  3. Add butter on top of the pork chops if using.
  4. Cook at 350°F for 20 minutes.
  5. Check the thickest pork chop with an instant-read thermometer.
  6. Continue cooking 3-10 minutes as needed, until the pork reaches at least 145°F in the thickest part.
  7. Rest 3 minutes. Season after cooking if desired.

Notes

Butter may brown, but at 350°F it should not be a deal-breaker. Pork chop thickness varies wildly, so thermometer beats clock.
