Vegetable Beef Stew with Potatoes and Carrots

A big-chunk, no-peel stew with beefy broth, sweet carrots, soft potatoes, and that old-school savory smell that makes a hotel room feel less like a hotel room. Built to eat as-is or ladle over rice.

Category: Main Dishes
Yield: 3-5 servings
Prep: 15 minutes
Cook: 35-45 minutes pressure cook plus release
Equipment: Instant Pot or electric pressure cooker; knife; cutting board; spoon or ladle
Tags: working draft

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2-2 lb beef stew meat or chunked roast
  • 3-4 potatoes, unpeeled and cut large
  • 3-4 carrots, unpeeled and cut large
  • 1 onion, chunked
  • 1 green pepper, optional, trimmed if sound
  • 2-3 cups beef broth or water plus bouillon
  • 1-2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 1-2 tablespoons soy sauce, optional
  • 1-2 tablespoons tomato paste or tomato base, optional
  • Black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, bay leaf, or preferred seasoning

Instructions

  1. Add beef to the Instant Pot with broth, Worcestershire, soy sauce if using, and tomato paste if using.
  2. Add potatoes, carrots, onion, and any other vegetables in large chunks.
  3. Use enough liquid for pressure cooking, but do not drown it unless you want soup.
  4. Pressure cook on High for 35-45 minutes, using the longer end for lean or tough cuts.
  5. Natural release at least 10 minutes, then vent remaining pressure.
  6. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  7. Serve as-is or ladle over brown or white rice.

Notes

Large vegetable chunks survive pressure cooking better. If using top round, London broil, or another lean cut, expect the longer end of the cook.
