Roasted Garlic Aioli

Rich, mellow, buttery garlic spread with a little lemony lift. Good on bread, potatoes, sandwiches, roasted vegetables, or anything that needs a savory glow-up.

Category: Sauces & Spreads
Yield: About 1 cup
Prep: 10 minutes
Cook: 40-50 minutes
Equipment: Oven, air fryer, or toaster oven for roasting garlic; bowl; whisk; fork; knife
Tags: working draft

Ingredients

  • 1 whole head garlic
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1 cup neutral oil or light olive oil
  • 1-2 teaspoons lemon juice or vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard, optional
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Roast the garlic until soft and golden, about 40-50 minutes at roughly 350°F to 375°F.
  2. Let garlic cool enough to handle.
  3. Squeeze roasted cloves into a bowl and mash smooth.
  4. Whisk egg yolks, lemon juice or vinegar, and Dijon if using.
  5. Slowly drizzle in oil while whisking constantly until thick and glossy.
  6. Whisk in roasted garlic.
  7. Season to taste and chill until ready to use.

Notes

Raw egg yolk is used here. Use pasteurized eggs if serving someone with food-safety concerns.
