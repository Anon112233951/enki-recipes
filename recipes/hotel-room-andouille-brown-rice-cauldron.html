Hotel-Room Andouille Brown Rice Cauldron

Born from pantry chaos. Refined by fond. Finished with pickle juice. Consumed with honor.

Description

A smoky, savory, tangy brown-rice stew bowl made with browned andouille sausage, carrots, onion, tomatoes, baked beans, mixed vegetables, Dijon mustard, Worcestershire sauce, Cholula Chipotle hot sauce, pickle juice, and taco-style cheese.

This is a method-based pantry meal. The key is browning the sausage, carrots, and onion deeply without scorching, then reducing the sauce until it becomes thick, rich, and stew-like.

Yield

Large batch. Enough for one person for about 3 days.

Estimated finished yield: about 9 to 12 cups total, depending on reduction.

Ingredients

Rice Base

  • 1 1/2 cups natural brown rice
  • 2 1/4 cups chicken broth
  • About 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • About 1 teaspoon soy sauce, optional
  • About 1/4 teaspoon Old Bay seasoning
  • Garlic powder, to taste
  • Onion powder, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Smoked paprika, to taste
  • Chili powder, to taste
  • About 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, optional

Sausage, Carrot, and Onion Base

  • 1 package Hillshire andouille sausage, sliced into coins or half-moons
  • About 5 small carrots, washed and sliced
  • Onion, sliced or chopped, amount to taste
  • Chicken broth, for splashing and deglazing
  • Worcestershire sauce, for splashing and deglazing
  • Small splash soy sauce, optional
  • Butter or oil, optional, only if needed

Sauce / Stew Mixture

  • 1 can diced tomatoes, with juice, about 14.5 to 15 oz
  • 1 small can Bush’s Original Baked Beans, about 8.3 oz
  • 1 can mixed vegetables, drained, about 14.5 to 15 oz
  • About 2 1/2 to 4 tablespoons Dijon mustard total, adjusted to taste
  • About 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce, plus more as needed
  • Cholula Chipotle hot sauce, to taste
  • Garlic powder, to taste
  • Onion powder, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Smoked paprika, to taste
  • Chili powder, to taste
  • About 1/2 teaspoon minced garlic, optional
  • Splash of Claussen pickle juice, added at the end
  • About 1 to 2 oz taco-style shredded cheese

Instructions

1. Brown the Andouille

Set the Instant Pot to Sauté, or use a stainless saucepan or skillet on an induction cooktop.

Add the sliced andouille sausage and cook until it begins to brown and render fat.

As the pan starts to dry out, add small splashes of chicken broth and Worcestershire sauce. Stir and scrape the bottom to keep the fond brown instead of black.

Continue cooking until the sausage has dark golden-brown spots.

2. Add the Carrots and Onion

Add the sliced carrots and onion to the browned andouille.

Continue cooking, stirring often. Use small splashes of chicken broth, Worcestershire sauce, and a tiny bit of soy sauce if needed to prevent burning and keep the browning process going.

Cook until the carrots and onion look caramelized, dark golden, and lightly charred in spots.

Remove the sausage, carrots, and onion to a bowl and set aside.

3. Cook the Brown Rice

In the Instant Pot, add the brown rice, chicken broth, Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce if using, Old Bay, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, smoked paprika, chili powder, and Dijon mustard if using.

Stir briefly.

Pressure cook on High for 24 minutes.

Let naturally release for 15 minutes.

Remove the lid and let the rice steam off uncovered for up to 30 minutes, or until the texture firms up and excess moisture evaporates.

4. Build the Sauce

In a 3-quart stainless saucepan, combine the diced tomatoes with juice, baked beans, drained mixed vegetables, browned andouille, carrots, and onion.

Add Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard, Cholula Chipotle, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, smoked paprika, chili powder, and minced garlic.

Simmer over medium to medium-low heat, stirring often.

As the sauce thickens, gradually lower the heat to avoid scorching.

Reduce for about 30 to 40 minutes, or until the sauce reaches a thick stew consistency.

5. Final Seasoning

Taste the sauce and adjust with more Dijon mustard, Cholula Chipotle, garlic powder, onion powder, or black pepper as needed.

Add a splash of pickle juice at the end for brightness.

Stir in the taco-style shredded cheese until melted into the sauce.

The finished sauce should be smoky, savory, tangy, spicy, creamy, and stew-thick.

6. Serve

Spoon brown rice into a bowl.

Top with the sausage, tomato, bean, and vegetable sauce.

Best tested serving ratio: 2 ladles brown rice to 2 1/2 ladles sauce.

Notes

This recipe is intentionally flexible. The original version was cooked by taste, color, texture, and reduction rather than exact measurements.

The key technique is using small splashes of chicken broth and Worcestershire sauce while browning the sausage, carrots, and onion. This keeps the pan from scorching while building deep browned flavor.

Do not rush the sauce reduction. The long simmer is what turns the tomatoes, baked beans, vegetables, sausage, mustard, hot sauce, and seasonings into one unified dish.

The pickle juice should be added at the end. It should brighten the dish without making it taste like pickles.

The cheese is not just a topping. Stirred into the sauce, it rounds off the heat, mustard, chili powder, and smoked paprika.

This recipe makes a large batch. It is best treated as a multi-day meal rather than a single dinner.
