Clean Wild Turkey Breast Lunch Meat

Description

Clean homemade wild turkey breast lunch meat made from unprocessed wild turkey breast. The turkey is dry brined, gently cooked in the Ninja Foodi Grill, chilled completely, and sliced thin for sandwiches.

This is a working draft recipe intended for sandwich meat, wraps, salads, crackers, or protein plates. It is not meant to be hot roasted turkey dinner meat.

Yield

About 1 to 1 1/4 lb sliced turkey, depending on trimming and moisture loss.

Preparation Time

15 minutes

Cooking Time

35 to 70 minutes

Inactive Time

8 to 24 hours dry brine, plus at least 4 hours chill time. Overnight chill is preferred.

Total Time

Best quality requires overnight dry brine plus overnight chill.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb wild turkey breast, trimmed
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp paprika, optional
  • 1/4 tsp dried thyme or poultry seasoning, optional
  • 1 tsp olive oil or melted butter, optional
  • 1/2 tsp Dijon mustard, optional
  • A few drops Worcestershire sauce, optional

Directions

  1. Inspect the wild turkey breast carefully. Remove shot pellets, feather bits, bruised or bloodshot meat, silver skin, hard tendons, ragged wound-channel meat, and any jelly-like or metallic-smelling damaged tissue. Pat the turkey dry with paper towels.
  2. Mix the kosher salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and optional paprika or thyme. Coat the turkey evenly on all sides.
  3. Place the seasoned turkey on a plate or tray, cover, and refrigerate for 8 to 24 hours. This dry brine helps the salt move into the meat instead of sitting only on the surface.
  4. Shape the turkey for slicing. If the breast is uneven, fold the thinner end under the thicker section to create a more even roast shape. Tie lightly with butcher twine if available.
  5. If you do not have butcher twine, place the turkey on parchment paper, roll or fold it into a compact shape, wrap it in parchment, then wrap it in aluminum foil. Crimp the ends to help hold the shape.
  6. Set the Ninja Foodi Grill to Bake or Roast at 250 degrees F.
  7. Place the wrapped turkey in the Ninja Foodi Grill. Cook until the thickest center reaches 160 to 165 degrees F internal temperature. For maximum safety, use 165 degrees F as the final target.
  8. Remove the turkey from the Ninja Foodi Grill. Leave it wrapped and rest at room temperature for 15 to 20 minutes.
  9. Place the wrapped turkey in the refrigerator. Chill at least 4 hours. Overnight chill is best.
  10. Unwrap the chilled turkey and slice thinly across the grain.

Cookware

  • Ninja Foodi Grill with Bake or Roast setting
  • Digital instant-read thermometer
  • Cutting board
  • Sharp knife
  • Measuring spoons
  • Parchment paper
  • Aluminum foil
  • Small tray or plate
  • Optional butcher twine
  • Optional food-safe gloves
  • Optional deli slicer or very sharp slicing knife

Notes

  • Working Draft: adjust salt, seasoning, cook time, and slicing thickness after the first test batch.
  • Wild turkey breast is lean and can dry out quickly. Gentle heat and controlled internal temperature matter more than the clock.
  • This recipe starts with 1/2 tsp kosher salt per pound. For 1 1/2 lb turkey, that equals 3/4 tsp kosher salt total.
  • For a stronger deli-meat flavor next time, increase to 3/4 tsp kosher salt per pound. For 1 1/2 lb turkey, that equals about 1 1/8 tsp kosher salt total.
  • The Ninja Foodi Grill is preferred over pressure cooking because it gives a slower, more controlled heat rise and a firmer, more sliceable lunch-meat texture.
  • Pressure cooking is better for shredded turkey, soup meat, gravy meat, turkey salad, or pulled-style turkey.
  • Do not slice the turkey hot if the goal is lunch meat. Hot slicing can leak juice and shred the meat.
  • Store refrigerated in an airtight container. Best quality is 3 to 4 days. Freeze small portions for longer storage.
  • Food safety target is based on USDA poultry guidance: 165 degrees F internal temperature.
