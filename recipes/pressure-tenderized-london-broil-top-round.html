Pressure-Tenderized London Broil or Top Round

A tough-cut rescue method: deep beef flavor, oniony juices, optional mushrooms, and a final hot finish if you want browned edges. Think practical roast-beef slices rather than steakhouse perfection.

Category: Main Dishes
Yield: 2-4 servings
Prep: 10 minutes
Cook: 35-50 minutes pressure cook plus 10-15 minutes release
Equipment: Instant Pot or electric pressure cooker; knife; cutting board; Ninja Foodi Grill, air fryer, or skillet optional for finishing
Tags: working draft

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2-2 lb London broil or top round
  • 1 onion, sliced, optional
  • 8 oz mushrooms, optional
  • 1-2 cups beef broth
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce, optional
  • Garlic, black pepper, onion powder, or preferred seasoning
  • 1 tablespoon butter or oil for finishing, optional

Instructions

  1. Season beef and place it in the Instant Pot with broth, Worcestershire, and soy sauce if using.
  2. Add onion and mushrooms if using.
  3. Pressure cook on High for 35-50 minutes, depending on thickness and toughness.
  4. Natural release 10-15 minutes.
  5. Slice across the grain.
  6. Optional: finish slices quickly in a hot air fryer, grill plate, or skillet with a little butter or oil.

Notes

This is a method more than a formal recipe. Lean top round gets much friendlier with pressure, but slicing across the grain still matters.
