Reverse-Sear Style Chicken Breast

A tender chicken-breast method aimed at juicy meat first and browned edges second. Savory, lightly crisp, and built for seasoning flexibility rather than a heavy breading.

Category: Main Dishes
Yield: 1-2 servings
Prep: 10 minutes plus optional marinating
Cook: 20-30 minutes
Equipment: Ninja Foodi Grill or air fryer; grill grate optional; tongs; instant-read thermometer
Tags: working draft

Ingredients

  • 1-2 chicken breasts, about 3/4-1 1/4 inches thick
  • 1-2 teaspoons vegetable oil, optional, for seasoning adhesion
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, or preferred seasoning
  • Optional marinade: Worcestershire, soy sauce, vinegar, mustard, or other savory liquid seasonings

Instructions

  1. Pat chicken dry. Oil lightly if using dry seasonings.
  2. Season or marinate as desired.
  3. Preheat the Ninja Foodi Grill or air fryer to 400°F.
  4. Cook chicken gently at 400°F until the center approaches 150°F to 155°F.
  5. Increase heat to 450°F briefly, or use the grill setting, to brown the outside if desired.
  6. Pull chicken when the thickest part reaches 165°F.
  7. Rest 5 minutes before slicing.

Notes

Vegetable oil can help seasonings stick and aid browning, but too much oil can protect the surface and reduce crisping. Thickness controls everything.
